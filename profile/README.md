# NYU Shanghai AI Interest Group

## ☕ About Us
NYU Shanghai AI Interest Group is a student-led organization under the CSDSE Department. We aim to build an open, active community for students interested in artificial intelligence, providing regular academic exchange and exposure to current research and real-world practice in AI.

## ☀️ What We Focus On
Our activities center on seminars and invited talks, covering a broad range of AI-related areas, including (but not limited to):

- Computer Vision (CV)
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Deep Learning
- Machine Learning & ML Systems

The content is research-oriented but accessible, suitable for students with different backgrounds who want to better understand modern AI.

## ☀️ Speakers & Seminars
We regularly invite speakers from leading universities and institutions, including Tsinghua University, SUSTech, and other top research and industry organizations, to share their work, insights, and experiences. These seminars are a great opportunity to learn about cutting-edge topics and to interact directly with researchers and practitioners.
